Complete Syllabus Breakdown

Structured 9-Topic Curriculum

Har concept ko step-by-step simple aur practical tareeke se design kiya gaya hai taaki har student bina kisi confusion ke master kar sake.

01 Foundation

ECG Fundamentals

ECG kya hai, kaise banta hai aur iske fundamental medical & electrical basic principles.

Module 1 • Basics
02 Conduction

Cardiac Electrical Conduction

SA node se AV node, Bundle of His aur Purkinje fibers tak electrical impulse ka pura safar.

Module 2 • Physiology
03 Morphology

ECG Waves & Intervals

P wave, QRS complex, T wave aur PR interval, QRS duration, QT interval ki complete practical samajh.

Module 3 • Waves & Segments
04 Leads

Lead System & Electrode Placement

Standard 12 leads ka core concept, bipolar limb leads, unipolar leads aur chest electrodes ki sahi placement.

Module 4 • Practical Technique
05 Calculations

Heart Rate Calculation

Regular aur irregular rhythms dono ke liye accurate, rapid aur easy heart rate calculation formulas.

Module 5 • Rate Estimation
06 Arrhythmias

Rhythm Analysis

Normal sinus rhythm recognize karna aur common arrhythmias ko accurately distinguish & identify karna.

Module 6 • Rhythm Diagnosis
07 Vectors

Cardiac Axis Determination

Electrical axis kya hai, hexaxial reference system aur LAD, RAD ko quickly calculate karne ki practical tips.

Module 7 • Axis Calculation
08 Algorithm

Step-by-Step ECG Interpretation

Systematic clinical protocol: Rate -> Rhythm -> Axis -> Intervals -> Morphology -> Clinical conclusion.

Module 8 • Systematic Reading
09 Pathology

Common ECG Abnormalities

Ischemia, Injury, Infarction patterns, Hypertrophy indicators aur crucial emergency findings.

Module 9 • Clinical Abnormalities
